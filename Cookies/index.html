Effective date: August 11, 2026

1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files or similar browser technologies stored on your device when you visit a website. They may support essential website functions, remember preferences, improve security, or help measure website usage.

2. Essential Storage

Our website may use essential browser storage required for technical functions such as inquiry-form progress, interface behavior, or temporary development features. Essential storage is used only where needed for the requested functionality.

3. Analytics

When analytics services such as Google Analytics are enabled, analytics cookies or similar identifiers may be used to understand aggregated website traffic, page usage, device types, referral sources, and performance. Analytics settings may be adjusted as our website configuration evolves.

4. Third-Party Technologies

Third-party services integrated into the website may place their own cookies or use similar technologies according to their own policies. Examples may include analytics, embedded media, communication tools, maps, or external service links.

5. Managing Cookies

You can generally control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some technologies may affect certain website features. Where required, additional consent controls may be introduced for non-essential cookies.

6. Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy when website functionality, analytics tools, or applicable requirements change.

Questions regarding our use of cookies may be sent to info@jncostech.com.
