Six-Step Development Process

01

Brand & Product Consultation

Understanding your brand vision, target market, product positioning, and specific formulation requirements through detailed consultation sessions.

02

Research & Formulation Development

Custom formulation design based on functional requirements, ingredient preferences, texture specifications, and market trends analysis.

03

Sample Evaluation & Optimization

Iterative testing and refinement to perfect texture, efficacy, stability, and sensorial experience based on your feedback.

04

Packaging & Production Planning

Packaging coordination, compatibility testing, manufacturing timeline development, and cost optimization strategies.

05

Manufacturing & Quality Control

Full-scale production with rigorous QC monitoring at every stage, from raw material verification to batch release approval.

06

Filling, Packaging & Delivery

Final packaging, batch coding, quality documentation, and logistics coordination to ensure on-time delivery.
