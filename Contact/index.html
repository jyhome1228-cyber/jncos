Frequently Asked Questions

What types of cosmetic products can JN COS TECH manufacture?

Our development and manufacturing scope includes skincare systems such as serums, creams, toners, essences, cleansers and masks, as well as selected sun care, body care, hair and scalp care, hydrogel and specialty treatment formats.

Do you support custom formulation development?

Yes. We support formulation development based on product concept, benchmark, texture, active ingredients, target market, claims direction and commercial requirements.

Can you support packaging coordination?

Yes. Depending on the project, we can coordinate primary and secondary packaging, compatibility discussion, component specifications, artwork readiness and production integration.

Do you provide pilot batch or sample development?

Sampling and pilot-stage development can be included depending on the formulation, packaging and project scope. Exact quantities and conditions are reviewed project by project.

Can you support international brand partnerships?

Yes. JN COS TECH works with international brands and partners and can support product development, manufacturing coordination, documentation discussion and long-term collaboration.

What information is needed to start an OEM / ODM project?

Helpful starting information includes your company and target market, product category, benchmark or concept, preferred ingredients or claims, packaging direction, expected quantity, target cost and launch schedule.
